Mathematical Modeling & Campus Route Optimization

How UI-FleetFlow coordinates multi-vehicle inter-faculty logistics using Capacitated Vehicle Routing Problem (CVRP) heuristics.

1 Clarke-Wright Savings

Computes cost savings obtained by combining separate depot round-trips into consolidated vehicle tours:

S(i, j) = d(D, i) + d(D, j) - d(i, j)

Pairs with highest savings are iteratively merged subject to maximum vehicle cargo capacity constraints (Σw_i ≤ Q_v).

2 2-Opt TSP Local Search

Once customer clusters are assigned to vehicles, the internal sequence is optimized via 2-opt edge-exchange operations:

Route' = R[:i] + R[i:j]⁻¹ + R[j:]

Eliminates crossing path segments, minimizing total travel distance and reducing urban transit delays.

3 Real-Time Telematics

Background asynchronous event loop interpolates street waypoints using great-circle Haversine navigation:

a = sin²(Δφ/2) + cos φ₁ cos φ₂ sin²(Δλ/2)

Broadcasts high-frequency GPS coordinates, heading bearings, battery consumption, and geofence drop-off triggers over WebSockets.

Mathematical Modeling: Capacitated Vehicle Routing Problem (CVRP)

Heuristic optimization formulations used to solve multi-vehicle logistics across the University of Ibadan network.

1 Clarke-Wright Savings

Computes cost savings obtained by combining separate depot round-trips into consolidated vehicle tours:

S(i, j) = d(D, i) + d(D, j) - d(i, j)

Pairs with highest savings are iteratively merged subject to maximum vehicle cargo capacity constraints (Σw_i ≤ Q_v).

2 2-Opt Local Search TSP

Once customer clusters are assigned to vehicles, the internal sequence is optimized via 2-opt edge-exchange operations:

Route' = R[:i] + R[i:j]⁻¹ + R[j:]

Eliminates crossing path segments, minimizing total travel distance and reducing transit delays.

3 Spherical Haversine Navigation

Real-time great-circle geodesic navigation calculates point-to-point distances and directional bearings:

a = sin²(Δφ/2) + cos φ₁ cos φ₂ sin²(Δλ/2)

Continuous telemetry event loop triggers automatic delivery arrival when vehicles enter within 70 meters.

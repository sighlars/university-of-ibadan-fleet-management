Capacitated Vehicle Routing Problem (CVRP) Optimization Engine

Formulations and mathematical heuristics applied to dispatch campus routes across the University of Ibadan

O(n² log n)
1 Clarke-Wright Savings Heuristic

Computes pairwise cost savings of merging two radial deliveries into a unified consolidated route:

s(i, j) = c₀ᵢ + c₀ⱼ - cᵢⱼ

Candidate savings pairs are sorted in descending order and greedily merged subject to vehicle weight limits.

2 Payload Capacity Constraints

Guarantees total consignment demand along any campus route does not exceed allocated vehicle capacity:

∑_{i ∈ Rₖ} qᵢ ≤ Qₖ

Vehicles are constrained by real utility capacities: Bajaj (85 kg), Keke (350 kg), Carry (750 kg), HiAce (1200 kg).

3 Spherical Haversine Navigation

Calculates great-circle geodesic distances and compass bearings between campus stops:

a = sin²(Δφ/2) + cos φ₁ cos φ₂ sin²(Δλ/2)

Telematics engine automatically logs order delivery arrival when vehicle reaches within 70m radius of stop.
